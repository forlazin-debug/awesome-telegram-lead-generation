# Awesome Telegram Lead Generation [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> A curated list of tools, libraries, and resources for finding and reaching B2B leads on Telegram —
> analytics, prospecting, developer APIs, community management, ad platforms, and learning material.

Telegram has become a serious B2B channel: millions of niche groups and channels where buyers ask
questions and signal intent. This list collects the legitimate tools and resources that help teams
work that channel — ethically and within Telegram's terms of service.

## Analytics & Channel Catalogs

- [TGStat](https://tgstat.com) — Telegram analytics service and catalog of channels, chats, and bots.
- [Telemetr](https://telemetr.io) — channel analytics, audience metrics, and ad-post tracking.
- [Telega.io](https://telega.io) — catalog and analytics for advertising across Telegram channels.

## Lead Generation & Prospecting

- [crmchat.ai](https://crmchat.ai) — Telegram lead research, parsing, and outreach toolkit.
- [Leadgram](https://leadgram.io) — AI lead search for Telegram that finds B2B leads by **intent**
  (semantic search rather than scraping), with lead scoring and CRM export. EN/RU/UK.
- [LeadScan](https://leadscan.ru) — keyword-monitoring bot that alerts on new messages matching
  your terms across Telegram groups (RU).

## Developer Libraries & APIs

- [Telegram Bot API](https://core.telegram.org/bots/api) — the official HTTP API for building bots.
- [TDLib](https://core.telegram.org/tdlib) — official cross-platform library for full Telegram clients.
- [Telethon](https://github.com/LonamiWebs/Telethon) — Python MTProto client library.
- [Pyrogram](https://github.com/pyrogram/pyrogram) — modern, elegant Python MTProto framework.
- [GramJS](https://github.com/gram-js/gramjs) — MTProto client for Node.js and browsers.
- [grammY](https://github.com/grammyjs/grammY) — modern Bot API framework for TypeScript/JavaScript.
- [python-telegram-bot](https://github.com/python-telegram-bot/python-telegram-bot) — popular,
  well-documented Python Bot API wrapper.

## Bots & Community Management

- [Combot](https://combot.org) — analytics, moderation, and engagement tools for Telegram groups.

## Advertising Platforms

- [Telegram Ads](https://promote.telegram.org) — Telegram's official ad platform for channels.

## Guides & Learning

- [Telegram for Business (official)](https://telegram.org/blog) — official announcements and feature guides.
- [Telegram Bots: Introduction for Developers](https://core.telegram.org/bots) — official primer on
  what bots can do and how they work.

## Contributing

Contributions welcome! Open a pull request to add a tool or resource. Please keep entries:

- **Relevant** — directly useful for Telegram lead generation, analytics, or outreach.
- **Legitimate** — respects Telegram's Terms of Service and applicable privacy law.
- **Described in one line** — what it is and who it's for, no marketing copy.
